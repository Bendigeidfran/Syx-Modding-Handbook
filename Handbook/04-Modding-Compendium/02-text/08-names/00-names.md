# text/names

This folder holds the lists the game draws names from: places on the world map, factions and their rulers, and individual subjects. The region and landmark lists are shuffled when the world is generated, and start again from the top if they run out.

## Sections

- [WorldAreas.txt](01-WorldAreas.md): names of regions.
- [WorldLandmarks.txt](02-WorldLandmarks.md): names and lore of landmarks.
- [world](03-world.md): names of factions and rulers.
- `nameset/`: names of subjects, covered under [Name-set files on the race sprite page](../../01-init/07-race/06-sprite/00-sprite.md#name-set-files).
