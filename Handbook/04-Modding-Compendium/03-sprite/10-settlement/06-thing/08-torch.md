# sprite/settlement/thing/Torch

Vanilla includes a `Torch.png` in this folder, 236 pixels wide and 94 high, but the game never opens it. Painting over it changes nothing.

The flames of torches and candles are drawn from [`Fire.png`](05-fire.md) instead, and the torches themselves are room furniture, drawn from the sprites the room's file names.
