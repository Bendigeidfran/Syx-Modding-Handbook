# sprite/settlement/map/Flower

Vanilla includes a `Flower.png` in this folder, 460 pixels wide and 62 high, but the game never opens it. Painting over it changes nothing.

The flowers on the settlement map are drawn from `_Flower.png` in [sprite/resource/growable](../../08-resource.md#growable) instead. Change that image if you want different flowers.
