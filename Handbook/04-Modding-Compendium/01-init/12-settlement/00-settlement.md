# init/settlement

The `init/settlement` folder defines what the city is built from, other than rooms: the roads and floors underfoot, the walls and ceilings of buildings, fences, fortifications, and the environment effects subjects feel around them.

The folder holds only sub-folders, and all the files in each one share a format.

## Sections

- [environment](01-environment.md): noise, light, space and the other effects a subject feels from their surroundings.
- [fence](02-fence.md): fences.
- [floor](03-floor.md): floors and roads.
- [fortification](04-fortification.md): defensive walls.
- [structure](05-structure.md): the materials buildings are made of.
